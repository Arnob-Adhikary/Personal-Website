Featured case study

Krusrak Bridge, Sneek

As-built BIM of an iconic timber bridge for structural analysis and renovation planning

Sneek, the Netherlands 1 cm accuracy Lead modeller & workflow developer

The renovation team needed an accurate as-built BIM model of this iconic wooden bridge for structural analysis. Our partner captured the bridge with a LiDAR scanner. Because of its unique twisted structure, the model had to match the point cloud to within 1 cm.

The problem

Revit's built-in tools (blend, swept blend, guided loft) and equation-based modelling in Dynamo were not flexible enough to reproduce the curved, twisted members at 1 cm accuracy.

  1. Extract geometry from the point cloud in Terrasolid as guiding lines for each twisted member.
  2. Generate masses from those lines with a custom Dynamo script.
  3. Convert to Revit families automatically and validate them against the point cloud.

The result was a semi-automated method that modelled the bridge's complex components successfully. It is an example of the kind of geometry-reconstruction problem I want to study more rigorously.

Geospatial mapping

Amsterdam Municipality

Large-scale MLS mapping to survey-grade NLCS drawings

Amsterdam, the Netherlands Team lead (6)
  1. Register and clean. Matched and aligned raw MLS scans in MicroStation Connect and Terrasolid, removing noise and scan-to-scan misalignment so the data could support survey-grade drawings.
  2. Extract features. Drafted road edges, curbs, utility covers and other surface elements into NLCS-compliant drawings using MicroStation, Terrasolid and AutoCAD.
  3. Automate QA. Built a suite of fully automated FME scripts for quality control and other workflow steps, catching geometry and attribute errors before review.

Outcome

The automation kept throughput high across 50+ deliverables while holding accuracy within 2 cm RMSE and drawing standards consistent.

Facility data

Drawing Management for Stichting Yuverta

End-to-end revision of a multi-site 2D drawing database

Yuverta's building drawings were outdated and inconsistent across its sites. Without reliable data, facility management, maintenance planning and safety compliance were all harder.

I led the revision of Yuverta's complete 2D drawing database, coordinating surveyors and drafters to update architectural, electrical, mechanical and safety floor plans at every location. The team worked in AutoCAD and Stabicad, using AutoLISP and FME scripts to speed up repetitive drafting and keep drawings consistent at scale.

Beyond drafting

Advised Yuverta on how to make better use of its facility data going forward.
