Research

In progress Independent research · with two colleagues from BECM, KUET

Multimodal Semantic Enrichment of As-Built BIM Through Attribute-Aware Asset Recognition and Inspection-Schedule Matching

Facility managers need as-built BIM models that show where fire-safety assets are, what they are, and whether they agree with the inspection records. My research adds fire-safety assets to existing as-built BIM by combining two sources of evidence: laser-scan panoramas and inspection schedules.

  • Attribute-aware recognition. Identifies not only the asset type but also the attributes inspectors care about, such as extinguisher agent type and exit-sign direction.
  • From images to 3D. Detections in 360° panoramas are mapped onto the registered point cloud and combined across scan stations to locate each asset reliably.
  • Matching to records. Assets are matched to inspection-schedule entries by type, attribute and space, then written into the IFC model with their source data. Anything unmatched becomes an audit list.

Undergraduate thesis

B.Sc., KUET

Supervisor:
Md Shahriar Hossain

Evaluation of Thermal Performance of a Wall-Integrated Evaporative Cooling System with Natural Ventilation in a Warm-Humid Climate

Evaluated how well a wall-integrated evaporative cooling system works in a humid climate. I simulated airflow and heat transfer in ANSYS Fluent (CFD) under a range of humidity and velocity conditions, then designed, built and tested physical micro-models to validate the simulations. The results showed a clear inverse relationship between cooling efficiency and ambient relative humidity.
